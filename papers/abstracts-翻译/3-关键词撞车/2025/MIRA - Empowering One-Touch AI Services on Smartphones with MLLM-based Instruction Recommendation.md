# MIRA: Empowering One-Touch AI Services on Smartphones with MLLM-based Instruction Recommendation
> 来源文件：MIRA - Empowering One-Touch AI Services on Smartphones with MLLM-based Instruction Recommendation.pdf
> 发表年份：2025
> 单位：Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab；Huawei Technologies Ltd.；Zhejiang University and Zhejiang University

## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）

The rapid advancement of generative AI technologies is driving the integration of diverse AI-powered services into smartphones, transforming how users interact with their devices. To simplify access to predefined AI services, this paper introduces MIRA, a pioneering framework for task instruction recommendation that enables intuitive one-touch AI tasking on smartphones. With MIRA, users can long-press on images or text objects to receive contextually relevant instruction recommendations for executing AI tasks. Our work introduces three key innovations: 1) A multimodal large language model (MLLM)-based recommendation pipeline with structured reasoning to extract key entities, infer user intent, and generate precise instructions; 2) A template-augmented reasoning mechanism that integrates high-level reasoning templates, enhancing task inference accuracy; 3) A prefix-tree-based constrained decoding strategy that restricts outputs to predefined instruction candidates, ensuring coherent and intent-aligned suggestions. Through evaluation using a real-world annotated dataset and a user study, MIRA has demonstrated substantial improvements in the accuracy of instruction recommendation. The encouraging results highlight MIRA's potential to revolutionize the way users engage with AI services on their smartphones, offering a more seamless and efficient experience.

## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）

生成式 AI 技术的快速发展正推动各类 AI 服务集成到智能手机中，改变着用户与设备的交互方式。为简化用户对预定义 AI 服务的调用，本文提出 MIRA，这是一个开创性的任务指令推荐框架，可在手机上实现直观的一键式 AI 任务调用。借助 MIRA，用户长按图片或文本对象，即可获得与上下文相关的指令推荐，用于执行相应的 AI 任务。本文的主要创新包括：1）基于多模态大语言模型（MLLM）的推荐流水线，通过结构化推理抽取关键实体、推断用户意图并生成精准指令；2）模板增强的推理机制，融合高层推理模板，提升任务推断准确率；3）基于前缀树的约束解码策略，将输出限制在预定义指令候选集中，保证推荐建议连贯且与意图对齐。基于真实标注数据集的评测和用户研究表明，MIRA 在指令推荐准确率上取得了显著提升。令人鼓舞的结果显示，MIRA 有潜力革新用户在手机上使用 AI 服务的方式，带来更无缝、高效的体验。
