# Evaluating Personalized Tool-Augmented LLMs from the Perspectives of Personalization and Proactivity
> 来源文件：Evaluating Personalized Tool-Augmented LLMs from the Perspectives of Personalization and Proactivity.pdf
## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）
Personalized tool utilization is essential for aligning large language models (LLMs) with user preference in interaction scenarios with various tools. However, most of the cur- rent benchmarks primarily focus on either per- sonalization of text generation or direct tool- utilizing, without considering both. In this work, we introduce a novel benchmark ETAPP for evaluating personalized tool invocation, es- tablishing a sandbox environment, and a com- prehensive dataset of 800 testing cases cover- ing diverse user profiles. To improve the ac- curacy of our evaluation, we propose a key- point-based LLM evaluation method, mitigat- ing biases in the LLM-as-a-judge system by manually annotating key points for each test case and providing them to LLM as the refer- ence. Additionally, we evaluate the excellent LLMs and provide an in-depth analysis. Fur- thermore, we investigate the impact of differ- ent tool-invoking strategies on LLMs’ person- alization performance and the effects of fine- tuning in our task. The effectiveness of our preference-setting and key-point-based eval- uation method is also validated. Our find- ings offer insights into improving personal- ized LLM agents. Our code is available at https://github.com/hypasd-art/ETAPP.

## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）
个性化工具利用对于使大语言模型（LLM）在各类工具交互场景中对齐用户偏好至关重要。然而，当前大多数基准测试要么仅关注文本生成的个性化，要么仅关注直接工具调用，未将二者同时纳入考量。本文提出了一个新颖的基准 ETAPP，用于评估个性化工具调用，构建了一个沙盒环境，并包含覆盖多样化用户画像的 800 个测试用例数据集。为提升评估准确性，我们提出了一种基于关键点的 LLM 评估方法，通过为每个测试用例人工标注关键点并将其作为参考提供给 LLM，从而缓解 LLM-as-a-judge 系统中的偏差。此外，我们评估了多个优秀 LLM 并进行了深入分析。进一步地，我们研究了不同工具调用策略对 LLM 个性化性能的影响以及微调在本任务中的作用。我们的偏好设置和基于关键点的评估方法的有效性也得到了验证。研究结果为改进个性化 LLM 智能体提供了洞见。代码已开源：https://github.com/hypasd-art/ETAPP。
