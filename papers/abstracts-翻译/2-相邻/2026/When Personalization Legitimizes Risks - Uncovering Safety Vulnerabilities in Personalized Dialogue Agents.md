# When Personalization Legitimizes Risks: Uncovering Safety Vulnerabilities in Personalized Dialogue Agents
> 来源文件：When Personalization Legitimizes Risks - Uncovering Safety Vulnerabilities in Personalized Dialogue Agents.pdf
> 发表年份：2026
> 单位：Harbin Institute of Technology
## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）
Long-term memory enables large language model (LLM) agents to support personalized and sustained interactions. However, most work on personalized agents prioritizes utility and user experience, treating memory as a neutral component and largely overlooking its safety implications. In this paper, we reveal intent legitimation, a previously underexplored safety failure in personalized agents, where benign personal memories bias intent inference and cause models to legitimize inherently harmful queries. To study this phenomenon, we introduce PS-Bench, a benchmark designed to identify and quantify intent legitimation in personalized interactions. Across multiple memory-augmented agent frameworks and base LLMs, personalization increases attack success rates by 15.8%–243.7% relative to stateless baselines. We further provide mechanistic evidence for intent legitimation from internal representations space, and propose a lightweight detection-reflection method that effectively reduces safety degradation. Overall, our work provides the first systematic exploration and evaluation of intent legitimation as a safety failure mode that naturally arises from benign, real-world personalization, highlighting the importance of assessing safety under long-term personal context. Our code is available at: https://github.com/MuyuenLP/PS-Bench.
## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）
长期记忆使大语言模型（LLM）智能体能够支持个性化、可持续的交互。然而，大多数关于个性化智能体的研究优先考虑效用和用户体验，将记忆视为中性组件，在很大程度上忽视了其安全影响。本文揭示了"意图合法化"（intent legitimation）这一个性化智能体中此前未被充分探索的安全失效模式：良性的个人记忆会偏差意图推理，导致模型将本质上有害的查询合法化。为研究这一现象，我们引入 PS-Bench——一个用于识别和量化个性化交互中意图合法化的基准。在多种记忆增强智能体框架和基座 LLM 上，相对于无状态基线，个性化使攻击成功率提升了 15.8%–243.7%。我们进一步从内部表示空间为意图合法化提供了机理证据，并提出了一种轻量级"检测-反思"方法，有效降低安全退化。总体而言，本工作首次系统探索并评估了"意图合法化"这一在真实良性个性化场景中自然涌现的安全失效模式，凸显了在长期个人上下文下评估安全性的重要性。代码见 https://github.com/MuyuenLP/PS-Bench。
