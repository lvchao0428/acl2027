# LLM 对话推荐中面向上下文漂移的数据高效适配
> 来源文件：Data-Efficient Adaptation to Contextual Shifts in LLM-based Conversational Recommendation.pdf
## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）
Large language model (LLM)-based conver-sational recommender systems (CRSs) have demonstrated strong capabilities in capturing user preferences and providing contextually rel-evant recommendations. Nevertheless, the rec-ommendation quality of the models frozen after training inevitably degrades under contextual shifts, such as changes in language and social trends. While periodic model updates are es-sential to maintain alignment with real-world preferences, training on large-scale data incurs substantial costs. This motivates data-efficient adaptation. However, existing data selection methods struggle to distinguish learnable sam-ples under contextual shifts. To address this, we propose Contextual Shift-Adaptive Data Pruning and Training (CAPT), a framework ag-nostic to underlying LLM-based CRSs. Specif-ically, we conceptualize a three-class data tax-onomy comprising familiar, valuable, and out-lier samples to formalize data behavior under contextual shifts. Based on this taxonomy, we design an importance score estimation scheme that quantifies a sample’s relative learnability for shift adaptation. Leveraging these impor-tance scores, CAPT prioritizes highly learnable samples and further guides shift-adaptive train-ing to actively steer the model toward current contexts. Experiments on three CRS bench-marks with real-world temporal splits demon-strate that CAPT outperforms baselines, match-ing or surpassing full-data fine-tuning perfor-mance using only 10–50% of the training data.
## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）
基于大语言模型（LLM）的对话式推荐系统（CRS）在捕捉用户偏好、提供上下文相关推荐方面展现出强大能力。然而，训练后冻结的模型在面对上下文漂移（如语言与社会潮流变化）时，推荐质量不可避免地退化。虽然周期性更新模型对保持与真实偏好一致至关重要，但在大规模数据上训练成本高昂，这催生了“数据高效适配”的需求。然而，现有数据选择方法难以在上下文漂移下区分“可学习样本”。为此，我们提出 CAPT（上下文漂移自适应的数据剪枝与训练），一个对底层 LLM-CRS 不敏感的框架。具体地，我们构建了一个三分类数据体系——熟悉样本、有价值样本与离群样本，用以形式化上下文漂移下的数据行为；并据此设计重要性打分方案，量化某样本相对于漂移适配的可学习性。CAPT 据此优先选取高可学习样本，并引导漂移自适应训练，使模型主动向当前上下文靠拢。在三个采用真实时间划分的 CRS 基准上，CAPT 优于基线，仅用 10–50% 的训练数据即可追平或超越全量数据微调的性能。
