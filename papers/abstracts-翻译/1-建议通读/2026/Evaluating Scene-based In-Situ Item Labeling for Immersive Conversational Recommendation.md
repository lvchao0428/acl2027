# 面向沉浸式对话推荐的场景化就地商品标注评估
> 来源文件：Evaluating Scene-based In-Situ Item Labeling for Immersive Conversational Recommendation.pdf
> 发表年份：2026
> 单位：University of Waterloo；Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering, University of Toronto and Department of Computer Science
## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）
The growing ubiquity of Extended Reality (XR) is driving Conversational Recommenda-tion Systems (CRS) toward visually immersive experiences. We formalize this paradigm as Immersive CRS (ICRS), where recommended items are highlighted directly in the user’s scene-based visual environment and augmented with in-situ labels. While item recommenda-tion has been widely studied, the problem of how to select and evaluate which information to present as immersive labels remains an open problem. To this end, we introduce a princi-pled categorization of information needs into explicit intent satisfaction and proactive infor-mation needs and use these to define novel eval-uation metrics for item label selection. We benchmark IR-, LLM-, and VLM-based meth-ods across three datasets and ICRS scenar-ios: fashion, movie recommendation, and retail shopping. Our evaluation reveals three impor-tant limitations of existing methods: (1) they fail to leverage scenario-specific information modalities (e.g., visual cues for fashion, meta-data for retail), (2) they present redundant infor-mation that is visually inferable, and (3) they poorly anticipate users’ proactive information needs from explicit dialogue alone. In sum-mary, this work provides both a novel evalua-tion paradigm for in-situ item labeling in ICRS and highlights key challenges for future work.
## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）
扩展现实（XR）的日益普及正推动对话式推荐系统（CRS）走向视觉沉浸体验。我们将这一范式形式化为沉浸式 CRS（ICRS）：被推荐商品直接在用户基于场景的视觉环境中高亮，并辅以就地（in-situ）标签。商品推荐已被广泛研究，但“应选择并评估哪些信息作为沉浸式标签呈现”仍是一个开放问题。为此，我们将信息需求有原则地划分为“显式意图满足”与“主动式信息需求”，并据此定义新的商品标签选择评估指标。我们在三个数据集、三种 ICRS 场景（时尚、电影推荐、零售购物）上，对基于 IR、LLM、VLM 的方法进行基准评测。评估揭示出现有方法的三点局限：(1) 未能利用场景特定的信息模态（如时尚中的视觉线索、零售中的元数据）；(2) 呈现了视觉上本可推断的冗余信息；(3) 仅凭显式对话难以预判用户的主动信息需求。综上，本工作为 ICRS 中的就地商品标注提供了新的评估范式，并指出了未来研究的关键挑战。
