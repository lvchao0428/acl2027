# DeepResearch Retail：电商领域工具增强型深度研究基准评测
> 来源文件：DeepResearch Retail - Benchmarking Tool-Augmented Deep Research in the E-Commerce Domain.pdf
> 发表年份：2026
> 单位：Anthology 未著录
## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）
Deep Research (DR) systems autonomously retrieve and synthesize information from web sources, however, industrial DR applications face a critical gap: effective integration of in-ternal tools with web search. In this work, we introduce DeepResearch Retail, an evaluation framework grounded in real-world e-commerce data for assessing Deep Research with tools (DR+Tools) in realistic commercial settings. The framework evaluates both factual faith-fulness and multidimensional response quality when reasoning over heterogeneous web and in-ternal data sources. We further present Hybrid-ReAct, a multi-agent architecture that demon-strates how collaborative reasoning and tool use can produce evidence-grounded answers. Experimental results validate our framework’s utility, showing improvements in agent’s perfor-mance when leveraging web-page information and multi-agent specialization.
## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）
深度研究（Deep Research, DR）系统能够自主地从网络来源检索并综合信息；然而工业界 DR 应用面临一个关键缺口：内部工具与网页搜索的有效整合。本文提出 DeepResearch Retail，一个扎根于真实电商数据的评估框架，用于在真实商业场景下评测带工具的深度研究（DR+Tools）。该框架在对异构网页与内部数据源推理时，同时评估事实忠实度与多维回答质量。我们进一步提出 Hybrid-ReAct，一个多智能体架构，展示协作式推理与工具使用如何产出有证据支撑的答案。实验结果验证了该框架的实用性：利用网页信息与多智能体分工后，智能体性能得到提升。
