# Pearl: A Review-driven Persona-Knowledge Grounded Conversational Recommendation Dataset
> 来源文件：Pearl - A Review-driven Persona-Knowledge Grounded Conversational Recommendation Dataset.pdf

## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）

Conversational recommender systems are an emerging area that has garnered increasing interest in the community, especially with the advancements in large language models (LLMs) that enable sophisticated handling of conversational input. Despite the progress, the field still has many aspects left to explore. The currently available public datasets for conversational recommendation lack specific user preferences and explanations for recommendations, hindering high-quality recommendations. To address such challenges, we present a novel conversational recommendation dataset named PEARL, synthesized with personaand knowledge-augmented LLM simulators. We obtain detailed persona and knowledge from real-world reviews and construct a large-scale dataset with over 57k dialogues. Our experimental results demonstrate that PEARL contains more specific user preferences, show expertise in the target domain, and provides recommendations more relevant to the dialogue context than those in prior datasets. Furthermore, we demonstrate the utility of PEARL by showing that our downstream models outperform baselines in both human and automatic evaluations.

## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）

对话推荐系统是一个新兴领域，在社区中受到越来越多的关注，尤其是随着大语言模型（LLM）的进步，使其能够对对话输入进行复杂处理。尽管已有进展，该领域仍有诸多方面有待探索。现有的公开对话推荐数据集缺乏特定的用户偏好以及对推荐的解释，阻碍了高质量推荐的生成。为应对这些挑战，我们提出一个新的对话推荐数据集，名为 PEARL，它由人设增强、知识增强的 LLM 模拟器合成。我们从真实评论中获取详尽的人设与知识，构建了一个包含 57k+ 对话的大规模数据集。实验结果表明，PEARL 相比以往数据集包含更具体的用户偏好、在目标领域展现出更强的专业性，并提供与对话上下文更相关的推荐。此外，我们通过展示下游模型在人工评估与自动评估上均优于基线，证明了 PEARL 的实用性。我们公开了数据集与代码。
