# LLM-REDIAL: A Large-Scale Dataset for Conversational Recommender Systems Created from User Behaviors with LLMs
> 来源文件：LLM-REDIAL - A Large-Scale Dataset for Conversational Recommender Systems Created from User Behaviors with LLMs.pdf
> 发表年份：2024
> 单位：Hangzhou Dianzi University；The Chinese University of Hong Kong；SalesForce.com

## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）

The large-scale conversational recommendation dataset is pivotal for the development of conversational recommender systems (CRS). Most existing CRS datasets suffers from the problems of data inextensibility and semantic inconsistency. To tackle these limitations and establish a benchmark in the conversational recommendation scenario, in this paper, we introduce the LLM-REDIAL dataset1 to facilitate the research in CRS. LLM-REDIAL is constructed by leveraging large language models (LLMs) to generate the high-quality dialogues. To provide the LLMs with detailed guidance, we integrate historical user behavior data with dialogue templates that are carefully designed through the combination of multiple pre-defined goals. LLM-REDIAL has two main advantages. First, it is the largest multidomain CRS dataset which consists of 47.6k multi-turn dialogues with 482.6k utterances across 4 domains. Second, dialogue semantics and the users’ historical interaction information is highly consistent. Human evaluation are conducted to verify the quality of LLM-REDIAL. In addition, we evaluate the usability of advanced LLM-based models on LLM-REDIAL.

## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）

大规模对话推荐数据集对对话推荐系统（CRS）的发展至关重要。现有 CRS 数据集大多存在数据不可扩展（inextensibility）和语义不一致的问题。为应对这些局限、并在对话推荐场景中建立一个基准，本文引入 LLM-REDIAL 数据集以推动 CRS 研究。LLM-REDIAL 通过利用大语言模型（LLM）生成高质量对话来构建。为给 LLM 提供详尽指导，我们将历史用户行为数据与精心设计的对话模板结合，模板由多个预定义目标组合而成。LLM-REDIAL 有两大主要优势：第一，它是规模最大的多领域 CRS 数据集，包含 47.6k 条多轮对话、482.6k 条话语，覆盖 4 个领域；第二，对话语义与用户历史交互信息高度一致。我们进行了人工评估以验证 LLM-REDIAL 的质量；此外，我们还评估了先进的基于 LLM 的模型在 LLM-REDIAL 上的可用性。
