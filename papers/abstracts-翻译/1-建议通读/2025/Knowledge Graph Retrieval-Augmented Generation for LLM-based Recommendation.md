# Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation
> 来源文件：Knowledge Graph Retrieval-Augmented Generation for LLM-based Recommendation.pdf
> 发表年份：2025
> 单位：The Hong Kong Polytechnic University；University of Birmingham；City University of Hong Kong；Baidu

## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）

Recommender systems have become increasingly vital in our daily lives, helping to alleviate the problem of information overload across various user-oriented online services. The emergence of Large Language Models (LLMs) has yielded remarkable achievements, demonstrating their potential for the development of next-generation recommender systems. Despite these advancements, LLM-based recommender systems face inherent limitations stemming from their LLM backbones, particularly issues of hallucinations and the lack of up-todate and domain-specific knowledge. Recently, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has garnered significant attention for addressing these limitations by leveraging external knowledge sources to enhance the understanding and generation of LLMs. However, vanilla RAG methods often introduce noise and neglect structural relationships in knowledge, limiting their effectiveness in LLM-based recommendations. To address these limitations, we propose to retrieve high-quality and up-to-date structure information from the knowledge graph (KG) to augment recommendations. Specifically, our approach develops a retrieval-augmented framework, termed K-RagRec, that facilitates the recommendation generation process by incorporating structure information from the external KG. Extensive experiments have been conducted to demonstrate the effectiveness of our proposed method.

## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）

推荐系统在日常生活中愈发重要，帮助缓解各类面向用户的在线服务中的信息过载问题。大语言模型（LLM）的出现取得了显著成果，展示了其在发展下一代推荐系统方面的潜力。尽管有这些进展，基于 LLM 的推荐系统仍受制于其 LLM 骨干所固有的局限，特别是幻觉问题以及缺乏最新、领域特定的知识。近来，检索增强生成（Retrieval-Augmented Generation, RAG）通过利用外部知识源增强 LLM 的理解与生成，受到广泛关注。然而，朴素的 RAG 方法常引入噪声，并忽视知识中的结构关系，限制了其在 LLM 推荐中的效果。为克服这些局限，我们提出从知识图谱（KG）中检索高质量、最新的结构信息来增强推荐。具体地，我们开发了一个检索增强框架，称为 K-RagRec，通过融入外部 KG 的结构信息来促进推荐生成过程。大量实验验证了所提方法的有效性。
