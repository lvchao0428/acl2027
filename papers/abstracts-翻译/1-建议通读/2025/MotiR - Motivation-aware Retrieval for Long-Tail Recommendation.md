# MotiR: Motivation-aware Retrieval for Long-Tail Recommendation
> 来源文件：MotiR - Motivation-aware Retrieval for Long-Tail Recommendation.pdf
> 发表年份：2025
> 单位：Alibaba Group；NA；Institute of Software Chinese Academy of Sciences；Renmin University of China

## 英文原文摘要（Original Abstract）

In the retrieval stage of recommendation systems, two-tower models are widely adopted for their efficiency as a predominant paradigm. However, this method, which relies on collaborative filtering signals, exhibits limitations in modeling similarity for long-tail items. To address this issue, we propose a Motivationaware Retrieval for Long-Tail Recommendation, named MotiR. The purchase motivations generated by LLMs represent a condensed abstraction of items’ intrinsic attributes. By effectively integrating them with traditional item features, this approach enables the two-tower model to capture semantic-level similarities among long-tail items. Furthermore, a gated network-based adaptive weighting mechanism dynamically adjusts representation weights: emphasizing semantic modeling for long-tail items while preserving collaborative signal advantages for popular items. Experimental results demonstrate 60.5% Hit@10 improvements over existing methods on Amazon Books. Industrial deployment in Taobao&Tmall Group 88VIP scenarios achieves over 4% CTR and CVR improvement, validating the effectiveness of our method.

## 中文翻译（Chinese Translation）

在推荐系统的召回（retrieval）阶段，双塔模型因其高效性被广泛采用，成为主导范式。然而，该方法依赖协同过滤信号，在建模长尾商品的相似度上存在局限。为此，我们提出用于长尾推荐的动机感知召回（Motivation-aware Retrieval for Long-Tail Recommendation），命名为 MotiR。由 LLM 生成的购买动机代表了对商品内在属性的凝练抽象。通过将其与传统商品特征有效整合，该方法使双塔模型能够捕获长尾商品之间的语义级相似度。此外，一个基于门控网络的自适应加权机制会动态调整表示权重：对长尾商品强调语义建模，同时对热门商品保留协同信号的优势。实验结果表明，在 Amazon Books 上 Hit@10 相对现有方法提升 60.5%；在淘宝&天猫集团 88VIP 场景的工业部署中，CTR 与 CVR 均提升超过 4%，验证了本方法的有效性。
